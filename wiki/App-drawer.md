# App drawer

Swipe up from the home screen to open it. Settings: **Home settings → Interface → App drawer** (colours under **Colors**, icon size under **Iconography**, columns under **Grids**).

## Hidden apps

**Hidden apps** lists every app with a checkbox. Ticked apps disappear from the drawer and from search. You can also hide one app from its long-press menu: **Customize → Hide from app drawer**.

## Drawer folders

**App drawer folders** groups apps into folders inside the drawer, without touching your home screen.

1. Tap **Create folder** and give it a name.
2. Tap the folder → **Manage apps** and tick the apps that belong in it.

**Hide folder apps** keeps apps that are in a drawer folder out of the main list, so each app shows up only once.

## Drawer tabs

**Drawer tabs** adds a row of tabs at the top of the drawer, such as Work, Games or Social, next to **All**.

1. Tap **Add tab** and name it.
2. Tap the tab → **Manage apps** and tick the apps for that tab. Rename it from the same sheet, or delete it with the bin icon.

**Remove apps from "All"** keeps apps that are in a tab out of the **All** tab.

## Behaviour

- **Close after launching an app** returns to the home screen, not the drawer, when you come back from an app.
- **Bounce at the edges** stretches the list when you scroll past the top or bottom. Turn it off for a hard stop.
- **Show the All tab** (under Drawer tabs) keeps a tab with every app. Turn it off to show only your own tabs.

## Duplicates

**Hide duplicate apps** shows one icon when an app has several entries with the same name, which some apps and cloned apps create.

## Layout

- **App drawer columns** and **Row height** set the grid.
- **Background opacity** sets how much of the wallpaper shows through.
- **Icon size**, **Show labels**, **Label size** and **Use multiple lines** control the icons in the drawer.
- **Remember scroll position** opens the drawer where you left it.
- **Suggestions** shows a row of apps you're likely to open next.
