# At a Glance

At a Glance is the line at the top of the home screen with the date and whatever's coming up. Settings: **Home settings → Widgets → At a Glance**.

## What to show

Each source has its own switch under **What to show**:

| Source | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Weather from the Google app** | The temperature from Google's own At a Glance. Tap it to open your weather app. |
| **Calendar events** | The event that's on now, or the next one within 24 hours, with its time and place. Tap it to open the event. |
| **Battery status** | Charging, and a warning when the battery is low |
| **Now playing** | The song or video that's playing |
| **Now Playing: songs heard nearby** | On a Pixel, the song Now Playing recognised nearby, for 10 minutes. Needs Now Playing's notification on and notification access for TheLauncher. |
| **Flashlight status** | When the flashlight is on |

When a source needs a permission (calendar for events) or Google's weather needs connecting, it asks the first time you turn it on.

## Weather

Weather comes only from the Google app, the same weather Google's own At a Glance shows. Its units and place follow the Google app's settings.

**Tapping the weather opens** picks the app that opens when you tap it. Left as **The phone's weather app**, it opens your phone's weather app, or the Google app's weather if there isn't one. It never opens the browser.

## Calendar

**Calendars to be shown** lists your phone's calendars. Turn off the ones whose events you don't want, such as holidays or birthdays. All-day events are skipped.

## Size

With the **Material card** style, **Size** picks how big the card is:

- **Compact: one row** shows the date; swipe it sideways to see anything else.
- **Auto: one row, two when there's more** shows just the date, and grows to a second row when there's something to show, such as an alarm, the flashlight or an event. It keeps the second row of your first home screen free for that.
- **Large: two rows** always takes two rows.

## Date and time

**Date**, **Time** and **Time format** choose what the first line shows. **Calendar** picks Gregorian or another calendar system.

## Turning it off

**Show on home screen** removes At a Glance from the home screen. You can still add it anywhere as a widget: long-press an empty spot → **Widgets → TheLauncher**.
