# Home screen

Settings: **Home settings → Interface → Home screen** (wallpaper under **Colors**, grid under **Grids**).

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/settings-home-screen.png" width="260" alt="Home screen settings">

## Grid

**Home screen → Home screen grid** sets the number of columns and rows, and how many icons fit in the dock. Changes show in a preview before you tap **Apply**. Up to 10 × 10 by default; **Experimental features → Increase maximum grid size limit** raises that to 20 × 20.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/settings-grid.png" width="260" alt="Home screen grid with preview">

## Adding and arranging

- **Add an app:** long-press it in the [app drawer](App-drawer) and drag it onto a page.
- **Add a widget:** long-press an empty spot → **Widgets**.
- **Add a page:** drag an icon past the right edge of the last page.
- **Add new apps to home screen** puts every newly installed app on the home screen by itself.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/home-menu.png" width="260" alt="Long-press menu"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/widgets.png" width="260" alt="Widgets">

## Locking the layout

**Home screen is locked** stops icons and widgets from being moved, removed or added by accident. To switch it from the home screen instead, add a lock button to the long-press menu under **Home screen → Pop-up menu**.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/settings-popup-menu.png" width="260" alt="Pop-up menu editor">

## Pages

- **Hide page indicator** removes the dots above the dock.
- **Fade on swipe** fades the icons of a page as you swipe away from it.
- **Infinite scrolling** lets you swipe from the last page straight to the first.
- **Return to first page on screen off** goes back to the first page whenever you turn the screen off.

## The feed

**Show feed** puts Google Discover (or another feed app) on a page to the left of your first page.

The Google app only shows its feed to specially built apps, so TheLauncher reaches it through a small companion app, **TheLauncher Feed**, which TheLauncher carries inside it:

1. Turn on **Show feed**.
2. Tap **Install TheLauncher Feed** and confirm. Nothing to download; the first time, Android asks you to allow TheLauncher to install apps. The companion has no icon and only works with TheLauncher.
3. Swipe right on your first home screen page.

**Feed theme** picks light, dark, or the Google app's own setting.

## Wallpaper

- **Scroll wallpaper** moves the wallpaper as you swipe between pages.
- **Wallpaper depth effect** zooms the wallpaper slightly when you open the drawer or Recents.
- **Wallpaper dimmer strength** darkens the wallpaper so icons and labels stand out.
- **Dark theme dims wallpaper** dims it only while the system dark theme is on.
- **Glow** adds an accent-coloured glow at the top and bottom of the home screen; **Glow opacity** sets how strong it is.
- **Top shadow** and **Navigation bar gradient** darken the top and bottom of the screen so the status bar and navigation bar stay readable on light wallpapers.

## Status and navigation bars

- **Show status bar** hides or shows the status bar on the home screen.
- **Hide navigation bar** hides the navigation bar on the home screen. Swipe up from the bottom edge to bring it back for a moment.

## Animations

**Animation speed** makes the launcher's animations relaxed, as set on your phone, or fast.

## Labels

**Label shadow** draws a dark shadow behind icon labels. Turn it off if your wallpaper is dark and you prefer clean text. Label size and visibility are under [Icons and look](Icons-and-look#sizes-and-labels).
