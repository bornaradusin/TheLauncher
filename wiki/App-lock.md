# App lock

App lock asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock before the launcher opens an app.

## Locking apps

Either:

- **Home settings → Interface → App lock**, then tick the apps, or
- long-press an app → **Customize** → turn on **Lock with biometrics**.

Your phone needs a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password). Without one, TheLauncher says so and doesn't open the app.

## Re-lock timeout

**App re-lock timeout**, under Locked apps, is how long an app stays unlocked after you open it, up to 30 minutes. At 0 it asks every time. Everything locks again as soon as the screen turns off.

## What it covers

App lock works whenever you open an app from TheLauncher: the home screen, the dock, the drawer, folders, search and app shortcuts. It can't stop an app being reached from Recents, a notification or another app, because Android doesn't let a home screen app control those.
