# Icons and look

## Icon packs

**Home settings → Iconography → Icon pack and shape** lists the icon packs on your phone. Pick one and every icon changes. Apps the pack doesn't cover keep their own icon.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/settings-icon-pack.png" width="260" alt="Icon pack picker">

To change one app's icon, long-press it → **Customize** → tap the icon and pick another from any pack. **Reset to default** undoes it.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/app-menu.png" width="260" alt="Long-press menu on an app"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/customize.png" width="260" alt="Customize sheet">

## Themed icons

**Themed icons** turns icons single-coloured to match your wallpaper colours, like Android's own themed icons. Every app is themed: an app without its own themed icon gets one made from its icon. An icon you picked for an app is themed too, and shows as picked when themed icons are off; a picture you chose always stays as it is.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/settings-themed-icons.png" width="260" alt="Themed icons">

## Icon shape

**Icon shape** changes the shape of every adaptive icon: circle, squircle, rounded square, teardrop and many more. Under **Custom** you can draw your own by setting each corner. **Folder** sets the folder shape separately.

## Sizes and labels

| Where | Settings |
|---|---|
| Home screen | **Home screen → Icons**: icon size, show labels, label size |
| Dock | **Dock → Show labels** |
| App drawer | **App drawer → Icons**: icon size, show labels, label size, use multiple lines |
| Folders | **Folders → Icons**: show labels, label size |

Sizes are percentages of the normal size for your grid, so 100% fills the cell nicely and smaller or bigger values scale from there.

## Renaming an app

Long-press it → **Customize**, then type a new name. Leave it empty to go back to the app's own name.

## Notification dots

**Colors → Theme and notification dots** shows a dot on apps with notifications. **Show notification counter** puts the number of notifications in the dot instead. **Dot size** makes the dot bigger or smaller, and **Hide dots on icons** keeps the notifications in the long-press menu without drawing a dot.

## Colours, theme and font

- **General → Colors** sets the accent colour, or follows your wallpaper.
- **Blackout** uses pure black backgrounds in dark mode for settings, the drawer and folders.
- **Color style** chooses how much of the wallpaper colour the launcher uses.
- **Font**: by default the launcher uses your phone's own font, as Hyperion does. **App names** and **Everything else** can each be changed; **Add fonts** takes OTF and TTF files.
- Colour settings offer **Automatic** (the launcher picks to suit your theme and wallpaper) and **Phone accent** (your phone's own accent colour).

## Wallpaper dimmer and shadows

Under [Home screen → Wallpaper](Home-screen#wallpaper): the dimmer, dimming in dark theme, the top shadow and the navigation bar gradient. The [dock shadow](Dock#look) is under Dock.
