# Widgets

TheLauncher has widgets of its own next to the ones your apps bring. Long-press an empty spot on the home screen, choose **Widgets**, and look under **TheLauncher**. Each one takes its colours from the wallpaper, uses the launcher's font, and can be resized by long-pressing it and dragging the handles.

| Widget | Shows | Tap |
|---|---|---|
| **At a Glance** | The date and what's coming up. See [At a Glance](At-a-Glance). | |
| **Clock** | The time in large digits, the date, and the next alarm when one is set | Opens your alarms |
| **Weather** | The weather now, the next hours, and the coming days | Opens your weather app |
| **Battery** | A ring for the phone and one for each connected Bluetooth device | Opens battery settings |
| **Now playing** | Album art, title and artist, with previous, play or pause, and next | Opens the app that's playing |

## Clock

The digits grow as you make the widget bigger. At one row high it shows the time only; taller, it adds the date and then the next alarm. It follows the phone's 12 or 24 hour setting.

## Weather

The forecast is for where the phone is. The first time, the widget asks for the location permission: tap it and allow. Only the rough location is used, rounded to about a kilometre, and it's sent to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), a free forecast service that needs no account. The forecast is refreshed when it's more than half an hour old.

How much it shows depends on its height: the temperature and conditions first, then the next hours, then up to five days. Temperatures are in Celsius, or Fahrenheit where the phone's region uses it.

This is separate from the weather in At a Glance, which comes from the Google app.

## Battery

The phone's ring turns green while charging and red at 15% or less. Tap the widget once to allow it to see nearby devices; after that, connected Bluetooth devices that report their battery (most earbuds, headphones and watches) get a ring too, up to four rings in all. A device that doesn't report a battery level isn't shown.

## Now playing

It shows whatever is playing, and keeps showing it while paused so you can carry on. The card's colour comes from the album art. It needs notification access for TheLauncher, the same access notification dots use; if it's off, the widget says so and a tap opens the setting.
