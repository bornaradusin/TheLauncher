# Settings

Long-press an empty spot on the home screen and tap **Home settings**. Settings open over your wallpaper and are grouped the same way as in Hyperion:

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/settings.png" width="260" alt="Settings categories"> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/TheLauncher/images/settings-more.png" width="260" alt="More settings categories">

| Category | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Colors** | Theme, accent colour, blackout, notification dots (size, colour, position, count), wallpaper dimmer and glow colours, dock, drawer and folder backgrounds |
| **Iconography** | Icon pack, themed icons, icon shape, icon background colour, and icon and label sizes, label lines and scrolling names for the home screen, dock, drawer and folders |
| **Typography** | Two fonts: one for app names, one for everything else |
| **Interface** | Rotation, updates and language, home screen behaviour, pages, status and navigation bars, the dock, the app drawer, drawer tabs, app lock, folders |
| **Grids** | Columns and rows for the home screen, dock, drawer and folders |
| **Widgets** | At a Glance, the search bar, widget corners and sizing |
| **Gestures** | What each gesture does, and haptic feedback |
| **Animations** | Animation speed, app closing animation, wallpaper scrolling and zoom |
| **Backup and restore** | Backups, saved layouts, one **Restore backup** for TheLauncher, Hyperion and Nova files, reloading icons |
| **Integrations** | The Google feed and its theme, suggestions, Seerr, experimental features |
| **About** | Version, updates, developer |

Each entry inside a category opens only the settings that belong there, so the same screen (for example Dock) shows its colours under **Colors** and its icon size under **Iconography**. Settings few people need, such as the dock background margins, tilt to swipe, widget sizing and the At a Glance provider, sit under a closed **Advanced** row; tap it to open them.
