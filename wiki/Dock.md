# Dock

Settings: **Home settings → Dock**.

## Icons and rows

- **Dock icons** sets how many icons sit in each row.
- **Dock rows** gives you one or two rows of icons.
- **Dock pages** lets you swipe the dock sideways for more pages of icons.
- **Show labels** shows app names under the dock icons. It's off by default.

## Search bar

The dock can show a search bar under the icons. Under **Search bar** pick **TheLauncher**, the **Google search bar**, or **Disabled** to hide it. More on it in [Search](Search).

## Look

- **Show background** draws a panel behind the dock, with its own corner radius, opacity and margins.
- **Dock shadow** draws a soft shadow behind the dock so it stands apart from the wallpaper.
- **Show dock** hides the dock completely when turned off.
